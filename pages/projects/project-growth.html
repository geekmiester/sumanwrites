Context

At Bajra Technologies, I owned two enterprise B2B SaaS modules serving customer teams with high implementation complexity. The challenge was not simply shipping features; it was setting a roadmap that balanced customer pain, product feasibility, and commercial upside.

The problem

  • Customer signals were scattered across support, sales conversations, and internal issues.
  • Leadership wanted clearer revenue impact, but the roadmap was created from urgency rather than evidence.
  • Product teams lacked a consistent framework for turning account insights into prioritised bets.

What I changed

  • Created a regular discovery rhythm using customer interviews, support history, and account reviews.
  • Turned raw feedback into opportunity-focused epics with measurable outcomes and stakeholders.
  • Worked closely with engineering and commercial leaders to sharpen prioritization and sequence delivery.

Priority impact chart

Outcome

  • Improved account expansion by aligning roadmap decisions to high-opportunity customer needs.
  • Created a clearer commercial narrative for product work across sales and delivery teams.
  • Reduced reactive prioritization by making customer context visible and actionable.

At a glance

  • Role: Product Manager / Product Owner

What mattered most

The key shift was making customer understanding visible enough to influence roadmap and commercial strategy, not just product delivery.
