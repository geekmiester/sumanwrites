Context

Teams were depending on fragmented documentation, scattered checklists, and informal knowledge to do their work. That created friction, slowed onboarding, and caused repeated cycles of clarification during important decisions.

The challenge

  • Information was spread across messages, documents, and project management notes.
  • New joiners had no structured path to understand how the team worked.
  • Process clarity was weak at the moments when teams needed to move quickly.

What I changed

  • Mapped the most important knowledge domains the team needed to operate effectively.
  • Designed a simpler structure around process, ownership, dependencies, and decision context.
  • Built a practical system that people could use every day, not just a documentation archive.

Outcome

  • Reduced time lost to repeated clarification and context-seeking.
  • Improved onboarding because the team had a clear, shared operational reference.
  • Created a more stable foundation for product and execution decisions across teams.
