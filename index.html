A little about Suman

Product thinking with a human point of view.

I’m most useful when a product problem has people, systems, and business pressure tangled together. I bring structure to the conversation, make the trade-offs visible, and help teams move from uncertainty toward something they can test and improve.

I started in software development, so I care about how ideas get built. I moved into product because I wanted to stay close to the bigger questions: what is worth solving, who it helps, and what changes after we ship.
