AI in product · 4 min read

AI where it helps.
Judgement where it matters.

Using AI to accelerate synthesis and first drafts while keeping product judgement human and accountable.

AI is most useful in product work when the task is repeatable, the inputs are available, and a person remains accountable for the output. That makes it a workflow tool, not a replacement for product judgement.

Where it helps

There are several steps where AI can create useful leverage:

  • Synthesising discovery calls and identifying recurring themes.
  • Creating a first pass of a PRD or user story.
  • Organising qualitative feedback into a structure the team can review.
  • Rapidly prototyping a flow to make a conversation more concrete.

The review step is the work

A first draft is not a finished product decision. It can miss context, overstate evidence, or quietly turn an assumption into a fact. I review outputs against the original source material and the decisions the team actually needs to make.

Speed is useful when it gives you more time to ask better questions.

Keep the boundary clear

Customer trust, private information, and sensitive business context need deliberate handling. Teams should understand what goes into a tool, what comes out, and who owns the final call.

A practical rule

Use AI to reduce repetitive effort. Use product judgement to decide what matters, what is true enough to act on, and what needs more discovery.
